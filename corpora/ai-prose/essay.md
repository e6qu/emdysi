# Sleep and Memory

Sleep arguably plays a pivotal role in memory. Researchers have found that people who sleep after learning remember more (Walker, 2017). It could be argued that sleep is the most underrated study tool.

The relationship between sleep and memory is intricate and multifaceted. During deep sleep, the brain seems to replay the day's experiences. This process is likely to strengthen the connections that matter.

Numerous studies suggest that even a short nap can help. Students who nap might possibly perform better on tests. Undeniably, schools should take note.

To some extent, the evidence is mixed. Some studies report small effects, and others report none [3].
