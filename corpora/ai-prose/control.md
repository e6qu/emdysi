# Moving the Office

We moved the office to Elm Street in March. The new building has more desks, a larger kitchen and two meeting rooms.

The move took three days. On the first day we packed the files; on the second we moved the furniture. The phone lines worked on the third day.

Rent is lower by twelve percent. The landlord fixed the heating before we arrived, and the bus stops outside the door.

If you need a parking permit, ask Dana at the front desk.
