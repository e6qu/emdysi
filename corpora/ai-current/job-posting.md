# Senior Backend Engineer

## About Us

At Fernleaf, we're on a mission to make financial planning accessible to everyone. We're a fast-growing, remote-first team of passionate builders who believe that great software can change lives. If you're excited about solving hard problems and making a real impact, we'd love to hear from you.

## The Role

As a Senior Backend Engineer, you'll play a key role in designing, building, and scaling the systems that power our platform. You'll work closely with product, design, and data teams to deliver robust, scalable solutions that delight our users.

## What You'll Do

- Design and build scalable, high-performance APIs and services.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to bring new features to life.
- Champion best practices in code quality, testing, and observability.
- Mentor other engineers and help foster a culture of continuous learning.

## What You'll Bring

- 5+ years of experience in backend development.
- Deep expertise in at least one modern language, such as Go, Rust, or Python.
- A strong understanding of distributed systems and cloud infrastructure.
- Excellent communication skills and a collaborative mindset.

## Why You'll Love Working Here

- Competitive salary and equity.
- Flexible hours and a fully remote team.
- A generous learning and development budget.
- A supportive, inclusive culture where your voice truly matters.

We're committed to building a diverse and inclusive team. Even if you don't meet every requirement, we encourage you to apply — you might be exactly who we're looking for!
