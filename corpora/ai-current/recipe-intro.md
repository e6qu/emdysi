# The Best Lemon Garlic Roast Chicken

There's something truly magical about a perfectly roasted chicken. Golden, crispy skin. Juicy, tender meat. A kitchen filled with the irresistible aroma of lemon, garlic, and fresh herbs. It's the kind of meal that brings people together — and this recipe makes it easier than ever.

Whether you're cooking for a cozy weeknight dinner or hosting a special gathering, this lemon garlic roast chicken is guaranteed to impress. And the best part? It requires just a handful of simple ingredients and minimal prep time.

## Why You'll Love This Recipe

- **Simple ingredients:** Everything you need is probably already in your kitchen.
- **Foolproof method:** Even beginners will get perfect results every time.
- **Incredible flavor:** Bright lemon and savory garlic make every bite unforgettable.

## Tips for the Perfect Roast

For the crispiest skin, be sure to pat the chicken completely dry before seasoning. Letting it rest for ten minutes after roasting is also key — it allows the juices to redistribute, ensuring every slice is moist and flavorful.
