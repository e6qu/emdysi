# Brightwave Announces Strategic Partnership to Accelerate Clean Energy Innovation

Brightwave, a leading provider of grid-scale battery storage, today announced a strategic partnership with Solace Power to accelerate the deployment of renewable energy solutions across North America.

The partnership brings together Brightwave's cutting-edge storage technology and Solace Power's extensive solar portfolio, creating a comprehensive offering that will help utilities and businesses meet their sustainability goals. Together, the two companies aim to deliver more than two gigawatts of clean energy capacity over the next five years.

"This partnership represents a pivotal moment for our company and for the clean energy industry as a whole," said Morgan Ellis, CEO of Brightwave. "By combining our strengths, we are uniquely positioned to address the growing demand for reliable, affordable, and sustainable power."

The collaboration underscores both companies' shared commitment to building a more resilient energy future. As demand for renewable energy continues to grow, partnerships like this one will play an increasingly important role in driving the transition to a low-carbon economy.

"We are thrilled to join forces with Brightwave," said Jamie Chen, President of Solace Power. "Together, we will unlock new opportunities and deliver real, lasting value to our customers and communities."
