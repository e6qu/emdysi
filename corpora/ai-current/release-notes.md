We're excited to announce Version 4.2, our most powerful release yet! This update is packed with new features, performance improvements, and quality-of-life enhancements designed to make your workflow smoother than ever.

- **Smart Sync:** Your projects now stay in sync across all your devices — seamlessly and automatically.

- Faster load times across the board
- Enhanced stability and reliability
- Streamlined onboarding for new users
