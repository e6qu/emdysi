I spent 10 years building startups.

Here are 7 lessons I wish I'd learned sooner: 🧵

1/ Your first idea is rarely your best idea.

The magic happens when you listen to your customers and iterate relentlessly.

2/ Hire for attitude, train for skill.

Skills can be taught. Curiosity and grit can't.

3/ Cash flow is king.

Revenue is vanity. Profit is sanity. Cash is reality.

4/ Done is better than perfect.

Ship early. Learn fast. Improve constantly.

5/ Your network is your net worth.

Every relationship you build today is an investment in your future.

6/ Burnout is not a badge of honor.

Rest isn't a reward — it's a requirement.

7/ Enjoy the journey.

The destination matters, but the people you meet along the way matter more.
