# Meeting Summary: Q3 Planning Sync

**Date:** Tuesday
**Attendees:** Product, Engineering, Design, Marketing

## Overview

The team met to align on priorities for the upcoming quarter. Overall, the discussion was productive, and there was strong consensus on the key focus areas moving forward.

## Key Discussion Points

- **Roadmap priorities:** The team agreed that improving onboarding should be the top priority, given its direct impact on activation and retention.
- **Resourcing:** Engineering raised concerns about bandwidth. It was noted that additional support may be needed to deliver on all planned initiatives.
- **Launch timing:** Marketing emphasized the importance of aligning the launch with the industry conference in October to maximize visibility.

## Decisions Made

1. Onboarding improvements will be prioritized for Q3.
2. The mobile redesign will be deferred to Q4.
3. A cross-functional working group will be formed to oversee the launch.
