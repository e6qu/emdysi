# Welcome to the Platform Team! 🎉

We're so excited to have you here! This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to hit the ground running. Don't worry — you're not expected to absorb it all at once. Take your time, ask questions, and remember: we've all been the new person before.

## Your First Week

Your first week is all about getting set up and getting to know the team. Here's what you can expect:

- **Day 1:** Get your laptop, accounts, and access sorted. Your onboarding buddy will reach out to say hello.
- **Day 2–3:** Shadow an on-call engineer and get familiar with our core services.
- **Day 4–5:** Pick up your first starter ticket and ship something small. Yes, really — in your first week!

## How We Work

We value ownership, curiosity, and kindness. In practice, this means:

- **We default to transparency.** Decisions, discussions, and documentation live in the open.
- **We write things down.** If it isn't documented, it didn't happen.
- **We assume good intent.** Feedback is a gift, and we give it generously and respectfully.

## Key Resources

You'll find the following resources incredibly helpful as you get up to speed:

- The **architecture overview**, which explains how our services fit together.
- The **runbook library**, your go-to guide for handling incidents.
- The **team handbook**, which covers everything from meeting norms to time-off policies.

## A Few Tips for Success

1. **Ask questions early and often.** There's no such thing as a silly question here.
2. **Don't try to learn everything at once.** Focus on the areas most relevant to your first projects.
3. **Take notes.** Your fresh perspective is valuable — if something is confusing, it's probably confusing for others too.

## We're Glad You're Here

Joining a new team can feel overwhelming, but you're not doing it alone. We're here to support you every step of the way. Welcome aboard — we can't wait to see what you'll build!
