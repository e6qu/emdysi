Today, we're thrilled to announce Ledgerline 2.0 — the biggest update in our history, and a major step forward in how finance teams work.

## What's New

**Smart Reconciliation.** Our new matching engine learns from your team's decisions, automatically reconciling up to 95% of transactions. No more spreadsheets. No more manual matching. Just results.

**Real-Time Insights.** Get a live, unified view of cash across every account and entity. Spot trends before they become problems, and make decisions with confidence.

**Seamless Integrations.** Connect Ledgerline to the tools you already use, from your ERP to your bank feeds, in just a few clicks.
