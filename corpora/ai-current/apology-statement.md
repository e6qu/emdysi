# An Update on Yesterday's Outage

Yesterday, many of you experienced an outage that prevented you from accessing your accounts for several hours. We want to start by saying, simply and sincerely: we're sorry.

We know how much you rely on our service, and we understand how disruptive this was. You trusted us, and we let you down. That's not the experience we want for any of our customers, and we take full responsibility.

## What Happened

During a routine database upgrade, an unexpected configuration issue caused a cascading failure across several of our core systems. Our team worked around the clock to identify the root cause and restore service as quickly as possible.

## What We're Doing About It

We are taking concrete steps to ensure this doesn't happen again:

- **Strengthening our upgrade process** with additional safeguards and automated checks.
- **Improving our monitoring** so we can detect and respond to issues faster.
- **Enhancing our communication** so you're kept informed in real time during any incident.

## Our Commitment to You

Reliability is at the heart of everything we do, and we're committed to earning back your trust. We'll be sharing a detailed post-incident report in the coming days.

Thank you for your patience and understanding. If you have any questions or concerns, our support team is here to help.
