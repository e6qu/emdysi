I almost didn't post this.

Three years ago, I was let go from a job I loved. No warning. No explanation. Just a 15-minute call and a laptop return label.

I was devastated.

But here's what I didn't realize at the time: it was the best thing that ever happened to me.

Because losing that job forced me to ask a question I'd been avoiding for years:

What do I actually want?

The answer wasn't a title. It wasn't a salary. It was impact.

So I started small. I mentored two junior engineers. I wrote about what I was learning. I said yes to things that scared me.

Fast forward to today: I lead a team of 40 incredible people, and I get to do work that genuinely matters.

The lesson?

Setbacks aren't the end of your story. They're the plot twist.

If you're going through something hard right now, I see you. Keep going. Your comeback is closer than you think.
