Whether you're heading to the office, catching a flight, or exploring a new city, this backpack has you covered. A dedicated laptop sleeve fits devices up to 16 inches, while a range of smart pockets keeps your essentials organized and within reach.

Crafted from water-resistant, recycled materials, the Everyday Carry Backpack is built to withstand whatever life throws at it. Reinforced stitching and premium zippers ensure it will be by your side for years to come.
