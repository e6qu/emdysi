# Frequently Asked Questions: Billing

## How does billing work?

Great question! Our billing is designed to be simple, transparent, and flexible. You'll be charged at the start of each billing cycle based on the plan you've selected.

## Can I change my plan at any time?

Absolutely! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan whenever you like. Changes take effect immediately, and any differences will be prorated — so you only ever pay for what you use.

## What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, as well as PayPal and bank transfers for annual plans. Rest assured, all payments are processed securely.

## What happens if my payment fails?

Don't worry — it happens! We'll automatically retry the payment and send you a friendly reminder. Your account will remain active while you update your payment details.

## Still have questions?

Our support team is here to help! Feel free to reach out anytime, and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
