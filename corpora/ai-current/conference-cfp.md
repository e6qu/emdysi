# Talk Proposal: Building Resilient Systems in an Unpredictable World

## Abstract

In today's fast-paced digital landscape, system failures are not a matter of if, but when. This talk explores how teams can move beyond reactive firefighting and embrace a proactive, resilience-first mindset.

Drawing on real-world lessons from scaling a platform to millions of users, I'll walk attendees through the key principles, patterns, and practices that make systems truly robust. We'll dive into circuit breakers, graceful degradation, and chaos engineering — and, more importantly, the cultural shifts that make them stick.

## Key Takeaways

Attendees will leave with:

- A clear framework for identifying hidden points of failure
- Practical strategies they can implement immediately
- A renewed perspective on resilience as a team sport

## Why This Talk Matters

Resilience isn't just a technical challenge — it's a human one. By bridging the gap between engineering and culture, this session empowers teams to build systems — and organizations — that thrive in the face of uncertainty.
